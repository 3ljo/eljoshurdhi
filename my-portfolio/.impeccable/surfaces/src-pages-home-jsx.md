# Surface brief: marketing site (all routes)

Scope: the whole site (`/`, `/work`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/contact`, 404), Persuade mode. Audience and product truth live in PRODUCT.md.

Job: a small-business owner or founder should understand the offer, trust the work, see the price schedule and start a project. Proof is the six real case studies; no testimonials or metrics exist. Constraints: keep siteConfig content, routes, light/dark toggle and the mailto lead form.

Memorable moment: the homepage drawn as a plan that plots itself, then builds.

Unresolved: real project screenshots (drawn previews ship until they exist); prices are flagged as placeholders in siteConfig.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is the construction drawing set for the visitor's next website: planned, priced in writing and approved before anything is built. It refuses the freelancer default of a dark page, one neon accent, glow orbs and a grid of equal cards.

OWN-WORLD: Two prints of one drawing: whiteprint (cool drafting paper, ink, diazo-blue linework and drafting grid) and blueprint (Prussian blue ground, white linework). Safety orange marks every action. Barlow Condensed lettering, Barlow text, Overpass Mono only for dimensions and figures. Sheet numbers, title blocks, callout bubbles, revision clouds, schedules and general notes are the component language; hairline rules, 2px corners, no floating cards.

STORY: The visitor sees their site planned before it is built and what every line is for, inspects real projects as as-built drawings, reads the package schedule and the five-step sequence, and starts a project.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Sheet A-001. Navigation is the sheet index across the top. Left 40%: the headline at 6rem condensed, the subcopy, Start My Project in orange and See the work. Right 60%: the proposed homepage as a line drawing with four numbered callouts; it plots itself, then its fills build in. Title block at the bottom right of the sheet.

FORM: Architect's drawing set, candidate 3 of 7 by resonance, seed key 7e014369. Signature interaction: plot, then build (stroke drawing, then clip-path fill), plus the white print / blue print toggle.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
