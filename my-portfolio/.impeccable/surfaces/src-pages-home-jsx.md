# Surface brief: marketing site (all routes)

Scope: the whole site (`/`, `/work`, `/pricing`, `/about`, `/contact`, 404), Persuade mode. Audience and product truth live in PRODUCT.md.

Job: a small-business owner or founder should understand the offer, trust the work, see the price schedule and start a project. Proof is the six real case studies with real screenshots of the live sites; no testimonials or metrics exist.

Constraints: keep siteConfig content, routes and the mailto lead form. The owner asked for an image- and video-rich site made with Higgsfield (credit budget about 100). The previous drawing-set world was rejected as "ugly, no images". Owner approved shipping to main once reviewed.

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-3-spread.png` (user picked "C: Open spread"). The direction itself was assigned by the roll after the user left the direction question unanswered twice.

Memorable moment: the open magazine spread, a living black-and-white portrait of Eljo facing a yellow offer page.

Unresolved: AI Receptionist and Nderto sit behind logins, so their screenshots show login screens; prices stay flagged as placeholders in siteConfig.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is an issue of a street-culture magazine whose cover star is the person who builds your website; every client project is a cover story. It refuses the dark-page, neon, glow-orb, equal-cards freelancer default and the quiet white minimal portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Flat saturated print fields (acid yellow, signal red, white newsprint, black ink) with one colour per cover story; ultra-heavy condensed grotesque for mastheads and headlines; grainy black-and-white editorial photography; open spreads on a single centre gutter, running heads, folios, numbered points; black pill buttons; no rounded cards, no glow.

STORY: The visitor meets Eljo on the left page, reads the offer and three promises on the right page, starts a project or turns to six cover stories with real screenshots, then reads the problem feature, the process, the packages and a closing call.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Running head across the top: ELJO · ISSUE 01 · TIRANA, navigation, black Start My Project pill. Left half: full-bleed black-and-white portrait of Eljo, a vertical signal-red ELJO masthead down its left edge. Right half: acid-yellow page, headline in condensed capitals near 6rem, three numbered promises, black Start My Project button and See the work link, folio 02. Cover Stories begin below the fold.

FORM: Street-culture magazine issue, candidate 4 of 7 by resonance, seed key b439f356, approved comp option 3 (open spread). Signature interaction: the living portrait (a subtle looping video of Eljo on the left page) and spreads that turn like pages as they enter.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
